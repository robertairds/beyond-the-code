# C6 Week — AI in Security 🛡️

📍 C6 Bank  
📅 10 June 2026  
🏷️ Artificial Intelligence, Fraud Prevention, Cybersecurity, Risk Management and Financial Technology

## About the event

An in-person panel at **C6 Week** focused on **"AI in Security: Real-Time Fraud Prevention"**.

The event brought together professionals working directly with security, fraud prevention and artificial intelligence to discuss how technology is being used to identify risks and respond to increasingly sophisticated fraud attempts.

## Main topics

- Artificial intelligence;
- Fraud prevention;
- Cybersecurity;
- Real-time monitoring;
- Risk management;
- Financial technology;
- Data analysis;
- Security and innovation.

## Highlights

One of the main takeaways from the panel was how **fraud prevention is becoming increasingly dependent on the ability to anticipate risks**.

As fraud techniques become more sophisticated, continuous monitoring and AI-powered solutions are becoming essential for identifying suspicious behavior and taking action in real time.

It was especially valuable to hear practical experiences from professionals who are directly involved in leading this transformation.

## Personal reflection

The discussion reinforced how **Artificial Intelligence is no longer just a technology for the future**.

With increasingly sophisticated fraud, the ability to anticipate risks, continuously monitor transactions and respond quickly has become an essential part of security today.

It was inspiring to hear from professionals who are working on these challenges in practice and to better understand how AI can be applied to protect people and businesses.
