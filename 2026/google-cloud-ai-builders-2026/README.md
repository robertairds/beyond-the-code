# Google Cloud AI Builders 2026 🤖☁️

📍 Brazil  
📅 2026  
🏷️ Artificial Intelligence, Cloud, Security and Innovation

## About the event

An event focused on artificial intelligence, cloud technologies and the different possibilities of building solutions with AI.

The experience helped me understand that artificial intelligence goes far beyond writing code.

## Main topics

- Artificial intelligence;
- Cloud computing;
- AI agents;
- Governance;
- Security;
- Identity;
- Observability;
- Orchestration;
- Responsible use of AI;
- Business applications of artificial intelligence.

## Highlights

One of the most interesting parts was learning more about the structure behind AI solutions.

Building an AI solution is not only about creating a prompt or writing code. It also involves security, identity, governance, monitoring and orchestration.

## Special mention

It was also interesting to see Vinicius Dias, from Guitarr, sharing how he uses AI in his daily work, even outside a traditional technology role.

This reinforced the idea that AI can support people from different fields and professional backgrounds.

## Personal reflection

This event expanded my understanding of AI.

I realized that the technology ecosystem is broad and that there are many ways to work with artificial intelligence, whether through development, infrastructure, security, data, governance or business.
