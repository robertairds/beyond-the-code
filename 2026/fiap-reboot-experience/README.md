# FIAP Reboot Experience 🚀

📍 FIAP, Brazil  
📅 18th June 2026  
🏷️ Artificial Intelligence, Career Transition, Technology and Soft Skills

## About the event

An experience at **FIAP Reboot Experience**, focused on technology, artificial intelligence, career development and the future of work.

The event was an opportunity to reflect on how technology and AI are changing the way we work, while also reinforcing the importance of human skills in professional development.

For someone going through a **career transition**, this perspective was especially meaningful.

## Main topics

- Artificial intelligence;
- Technology and coding;
- Career transition;
- Professional development;
- Communication;
- Human skills;
- Adaptability;
- Future of work.

## Highlights

One of the biggest takeaways from the event was a simple but powerful idea:

> **Companies can teach you the code, but your humanity is something only you bring.**

Technology and artificial intelligence can help us increase productivity and improve results, but our ability to communicate, collaborate, adapt and understand people remains a fundamental differentiator.

The event also reinforced that AI should be seen as a tool to **enhance our capabilities**, rather than replace the human perspective behind the decisions.

## People and connections

The event was also an opportunity to connect with other professionals, exchange experiences and hear different perspectives about technology and career development.

These conversations were especially valuable while navigating a career transition and building a new path in technology.

## Personal reflection

As someone going through a **career transition**, this event had a special meaning for me.

We often hear about the importance of learning new technologies, programming languages and artificial intelligence. But one of my biggest takeaways was remembering that our previous experiences and human skills are also valuable assets.

Our background, communication skills, ability to collaborate and the way we connect with people are part of what makes us unique.

> **AI can help us navigate the journey, but we are still the ones steering the ship.** 🚀

I left FIAP Reboot Experience feeling inspired and even more confident that I am on the right path.

Thank you, **FIAP**, for the learnings and for such an inspiring experience! ✨
