# São Paulo Innovation Week — SPIW 💜

📍 São Paulo  
📅 14 May 2026  
🏷️ Technology, Innovation, Networking and Professional Development

## About the event

An experience at the first edition of **São Paulo Innovation Week (SPIW)**, bringing together technology, innovation, companies, professionals and people interested in the future of the industry.

This was my **first tech event**, which made the experience even more special. I was excited and had high expectations, but the event exceeded them.

## Main topics

- Technology;
- Innovation;
- Digital transformation;
- Technology companies;
- Professional development;
- Networking;
- Knowledge sharing;
- Career development.

## Highlights

One of the most exciting parts of the event was being able to see **projects, talks and company booths in person**, especially from companies and brands that I already admired.

The event also provided an opportunity to learn more about different technologies, listen to professionals with years of experience and understand different perspectives about the technology industry.

## People and connections

The most valuable part of the event was the opportunity to connect with people.

Talking to professionals who have been working in technology for years, hearing their experiences and receiving advice made the experience even more meaningful.

These conversations helped me gain new perspectives and reinforced my belief that I am on the right path in my career transition into technology.

## Personal reflection

This event was a very important milestone for me because it was my **first experience at a technology event**.

I went in with a lot of excitement and high expectations, and I left even more motivated.

Although technology was at the center of the event, what stood out to me the most was the **human connection** — the conversations, experiences, advice and connections made throughout the day.

It was inspiring to be surrounded by people who are passionate about technology and to see how many different paths can lead to a career in this area.

I really enjoyed this new experience and I'm already looking forward to the next events! 🚀
