# C6 Technology Talk 💛

📍 C6 Bank  
📅 27th August 2026  
🏷️ Technology, Business, Innovation and Career

## About the talk

A talk with Nelson Novaes Neto, CTO of C6 Bank, about his professional journey, the creation of C6 Bank and the relationship between technology, business and innovation.

## Main topics

- Professional journey;
- Technology and business;
- Innovation;
- Building products and solutions;
- Company growth;
- Challenges in technology;
- Learning through practice.

## Highlight

One of the phrases that stayed with me was the idea of starting with the “speedometer of a bicycle”.

For me, this means starting with what we have, gaining experience and increasing our speed along the way instead of waiting until we feel completely ready.

## Personal reflection

This talk was especially meaningful because I am beginning my own journey in technology.

It reminded me that every professional journey starts somewhere and that growth comes from taking the first step, learning and continuing to move forward.
