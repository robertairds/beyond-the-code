# SouJava Meetup + Oracle 🚀

📍 Oracle  
📅 17 June 2026  
🏷️ Java, Artificial Intelligence, Data Structures, High Performance and Technology Community

## About the event

An in-person meetup organized by the **SouJava community** at the **Oracle** building, bringing together professionals and technology enthusiasts for an evening of talks, knowledge sharing and networking.

The event featured three great talks covering different aspects of technology, from career development and artificial intelligence to data structures and high-performance solutions.

## Main topics

- Java;
- Artificial intelligence;
- Software development;
- Career development;
- Data structures;
- High-performance programming;
- Technology communities;
- Knowledge sharing;
- Networking.

## Highlights

### 👩‍💻 Career and Software Development

[Renata R. Silva](https://www.linkedin.com/in/renatarsilvax/) shared her journey as a developer, including the challenges and lessons that helped her reach a major technology company.

Her story resonated with my own career journey and was a great source of motivation, especially while building my path in technology.

### 🤖 Artificial Intelligence

[Vinicius Senger](https://www.linkedin.com/in/viniciussenger/) brought practical insights about **Artificial Intelligence** and how we can use it in our daily lives and professional routines.

His experience changed the way I think about applying AI as a tool for learning, productivity and continuous improvement.

And, of course, his experience of living in a motorhome definitely made the talk even more interesting! 🚐

### ⚡ Data Structures and High Performance

[Renan B. Castiglioni](https://www.linkedin.com/in/renanberaldi/) closed the event with a talk focused on **data structures and high performance**.

It was a great reminder that strong fundamentals are essential for building efficient solutions.

Writing code that simply works is not always enough. Understanding the fundamentals is what allows us to design solutions that are efficient, scalable and reliable.

This meetup was a great combination of **technical knowledge, career inspiration and community**.
