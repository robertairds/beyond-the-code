# Oracle Innovators Club + Oracle ACE 🧡

📍 Oracle Innovation Center  
📅 28th July 2026
🏷️ Oracle, Artificial Intelligence, Innovation and Technology Community

## About the event

An experience at the **Oracle Innovation Center**, the first Oracle innovation center in Latin America, with a guided visit, presentations and conversations about technology, artificial intelligence and innovation.

The event was promoted by the **Oracle ACE community** and was also an opportunity to learn more about how Oracle applies technology to different areas, including healthcare, agribusiness, payments and Formula 1.

## Main topics

- Artificial intelligence;
- Oracle technologies;
- Innovation;
- Cloud and technology solutions;
- Business applications;
- Technology communities;
- Knowledge sharing;
- Networking and professional development.

## Highlights

One of the highlights was the **guided visit to the Oracle Innovation Center**, led by [Pedro Izzo Palauso](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pedro-izzo-palauso/).

The visit provided an opportunity to see different projects and understand how innovation and technology are being applied to real-world challenges.

Another important part of the event was connecting with members of the **Oracle ACE community** and learning more about how the community encourages knowledge sharing, collaboration and professional development.
