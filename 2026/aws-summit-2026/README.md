# AWS Summit 2026 ☁️

📍 São Paulo, Brazil  
📅 3rd September 2026  
🏷️ Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence and Innovation

## About the event

My first experience at AWS Summit.

The event brought together talks, demonstrations, technology companies, professionals and opportunities to learn more about cloud computing, artificial intelligence and innovation.

## Highlights

- Cloud computing and its impact on businesses;
- Artificial intelligence applications;
- Technology, innovation and business strategy;
- Different solutions and possibilities within the cloud ecosystem;
- The importance of networking and knowledge exchange.

## Connections

During the event, I had the opportunity to:

- Meet technology professionals;
- Reconnect with friends from Generation Brasil;
- Meet people from different areas of technology;
- Learn more about the partnership between C6 Bank and AWS.

## Personal reflection

One of the things I enjoyed the most was realizing that technology is much bigger than programming.

It also involves people, business, innovation, communication and continuous learning.

This experience made me even more excited about the path I am building in technology.
